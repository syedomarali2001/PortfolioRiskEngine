PURPOSE
-------
V1–V7 provided validated historical prices, CAD/USD conversion, allocation,
performance, rebalancing/backtesting, risk statistics, walk-forward portfolio
construction, and conservative current ETF constituent look-through.

V8 adds an accessible, explainable RESEARCH BACKEND that connects those
measurements: objectively described strengths/weaknesses versus both common
S&P 500 benchmark proxies, user-chosen what-if allocations and strategy
priorities, attribution and sector/ETF drill-downs, selected historical
scenario/sensitivity tools, and individual-stock fundamentals/DCF.

The result is reproducible JSON and command-line analysis. It is not yet a
graphical product, trading algorithm, investment adviser, paid market-data
service, or guarantee of improved future portfolio outcomes.

IMPLEMENTED FEATURES
--------------------
1. insights: compare a hypothetical portfolio with SPY (cap-weight S&P 500
   ETF) and RSP (equal-weight S&P 500 ETF) on common observed trading dates,
   identical reporting currency and consistent returns methodology.
   Metrics include CAGR, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, drawdown, Expected
   Shortfall at 95%, Calmar, benchmark-relative beta, correlation, tracking
   error and information ratio.
2. Evidence-based metric diagnostics: positive, negative, neutral, or
   unavailable per metric and benchmark, with observed values, signed
   favorable difference, specified materiality threshold, adequate-period
   checks, metric definition and rationale. Neutral = an evaluated, small
   difference; unavailable = truly missing/undefined data, NOT neutral.
   Strengths and weaknesses remain separate instead of a vague net score.
3. Risk attribution: direct holding weights, standalone volatility,
   contribution to total portfolio volatility, and share of total risk.
4. What-if user allocations: reject missing tickers or total weights !=100%;
   contrast adjusted scenarios with the input under the same historical
   data and disclose performance/risk tradeoffs.
5. Candidate strategy selection: input/equal/inverse-volatility/minimum-
   variance/blended candidates ranked ONLY using the earlier 70% of the
   shared history according to user's metric priorities. The later 30%
   holdout is reported separately, including whether each selected
   objective actually improved, worsened or changed immaterially.
   A candidate's training success is NOT presented as realized alpha.
6. Historical-event replay when a complete event window exists; starting-
   date sensitivity for available 1/3/5-year windows; reproducible
   5-day-block bootstrap Monte Carlo with uncertainty limitations.
7. Optional USD versus CAD reporting-currency comparison on aligned FX
   history. Does not imply investment hedging or revenue-currency exposure.
8. exposure --drilldown: top holdings per supported ETF, effective whole-
   portfolio positions, sector contribution by investment, per-company
   contributors when independently classified, overlap stock contributors,
   and named/unattributed missing coverage. Optional capped (1..200)
   best-effort cached Yahoo constituent metadata lookups. Independent fund
   aggregate sectors are not invented into per-constituent labels.
9. fundamentals: manually supplied or best-effort Yahoo company statements,
   internally reconciled free cash flow, net debt, EV, P/E, P/FCF,
   EV/EBITDA, FCF margins, optional user-selected peer multiples (when 2+
   usable peers per multiple), and assumption-driven five-year DCF scenarios.
   Unavailable/negative inputs are not replaced with fake valuations.

USER-FACING SAFETY CONTRACT / KNOWN LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------------------------
- V8 uses research/backtest assumptions, not actual portfolio cash flows or
  realized historical account returns. Daily constant weight implicitly
  rebalances every session WITHOUT cost, spreads or taxes in this V8 mode.
- SPY and RSP are investable ETF proxies, NOT fee-free underlying indexes.
  Same-date comparisons use only their intersection of observed sessions;
  other historical calendars may differ.
- Ratio calculations here used 0% annual risk-free rate; this is an input,
  not a claim that the actual risk-free rate was zero in 2022–2026.
- Alert sentiment thresholds are transparent MATERIALITY heuristics, not
  universally accepted quality cutoffs or proof of statistical significance.
- The candidate search is SMALL, long-only and retrospective; it does not
  optimize globally. The holdout selection is past-only, but the chosen
  universe and candidate grid can still be affected by hindsight bias.
- DCF scenarios are dependent on hypothetical growth/discount rates and
  historical financial statement availability. They must never be treated
  as definitive price targets or automatic positive/negative invest calls.
  Banks and other financial firms may require alternative valuation models.
- Not verified: live U.S./Canadian stock coverage beyond AAPL for V8
  fundamentals, live peer-group accuracy, current index constituents,
  point-in-time archived fundamentals, transaction/broker integrations,
  fully classified Canadian ETF holdings, bond/derivative specialization.
- With V7 ETF look-through, not all holdings or constituent sectors are
  known; unknown portions remain visible, not redistributed or guessed.
- Third-party data via Yahoo/yfinance is unofficial and for personal
  research; confirm licensing/attribution before any public hosted app.
- No GUI in V8: V9 will expose the same core backend via Streamlit.

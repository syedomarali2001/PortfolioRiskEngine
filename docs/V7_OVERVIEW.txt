PORTFOLIO RISK ENGINE — V7 COMPLETION OVERVIEW
===============================================
Version: V7.3 — ETF Exposure, Market Coverage and Support Guardrails
Live verification: user's U.S. ETF and portfolio-exposure JSON reports,
2026-11-07; 380 deterministic regression tests also passed in the project
working copy on 2026-11-07.
Status: COMPLETE for V7's declared, limited scope. Market-wide completeness,
all ETF constituents, and historical as-of holdings are NOT claimed.
Public release remains deferred until the planned V9 interface/polish.

PURPOSE
-------
V1–V6 established historical prices and caching, CAD/USD valuation,
portfolio weights, performance, backtesting, VaR/Expected Shortfall, stress
tests, and strategy construction. V7 adds *current/last-reported* portfolio
exposure intelligence and transparent data-coverage limits. The goal is
honest analysis of supported investment types rather than false full-market
coverage.

DELIVERED THROUGH V7.0–V7.3
--------------------------
1. Current portfolio look-through using direct securities and fund
   constituents, with identified/unidentified NAV shares, top underlying
   positions, sector/country/asset-class composition, and overlap lower bounds.
   Includes synthetic/manual constituent inputs, nested holdings resolution,
   dated source disclosures and quality warnings.
2. Independent benchmark ETF proxy regressions for historical portfolio
   returns (SPY/MTUM examples). These are descriptive correlations/betas,
   not orthogonal factor regressions or demonstrated trading alpha.
3. Automatic U.S. ETF holdings retrieval via the free TickerInside API,
   without hardcoding individual issuers; source attribution, disclosure
   dates, weight reconciliation and optional 24-hour request caching.
4. Yahoo-backed market-pricing checks and automatic company classifications
   for U.S. and Canadian direct stocks: sector, industry, company location,
   listing exchange, trading currency, security type and company name.
5. Diagnostic commands for a 30-asset mixed universe, 241-fund U.S. ETF
   universe, 110-stock U.S./Canadian universe, and combined capability JSON.
6. V7.3 safety contract: `supported`, `partial`, `price_only`, `unverified`;
   optional `--strict-exposure` rejection of insufficient detailed exposure.
   `price_only` DOES NOT imply missing historical price data.
7. Equity-validation improvement: issuer-classified stock funds may now
   qualify even when the provider's generic holdings-kind label is unknown,
   but only with corroborating contemporaneous provider classification,
   stock count, sector coverage and other-fund screening. Cash is separated;
   futures, swaps, bonds and unverified holdings are excluded from stock
   overlap. Sector taxonomies are normalized across direct stocks/funds.

OFFICIAL V7 SUPPORT CONTRACT
-----------------------------
- U.S. and Canadian individual equities: historical price risk where
  sufficient observations exist; direct sector/industry/company location
  when Yahoo metadata is returned. Specific audit coverage: 110/110.
- U.S.-LISTED EQUITY ETFs (not simply USD-denominated ETFs): holdings,
  sector/country breakdowns and stock overlap ONLY when automatic fund
  validation passes; otherwise partial/price-only/unverified with reason.
- Canadian-listed ETFs: price-based metrics where data permit; NO automatic
  full underlying constituent look-through in V7.
- Physical bullion / selected spot crypto ETPs: price-based risk and
  identifiable metal/coin exposure where the source supports it. No
  company-sector classification for physical assets.
- Direct crypto USD pairs: price risk and coin identity when supported by
  the price adapter; no invented company industry or domicile.
- Bond ETFs / individual fixed income, futures, swaps, leveraged/inverse
  funds, covered-call option exposures and futures-based commodity pools:
  detailed constituent, duration, credit, leverage and derivative exposure
  OUT OF SCOPE. Price-based risk may still work where suitable prices exist.
- Unknown or unsupported assets: warn with explicit reason. In ordinary
  exposure mode, show unknown/partial weight and provenance; in strict
  exposure mode reject incomplete, stale, unsafe or unverified results.

ACCURACY, LICENSING, AND METHOD LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------
- TickerInside holdings are dated last-known snapshots, NOT time-machine
  ETF composition. Do not inject current constituents into historic
  portfolio backtests as if they were contemporaneously known.
- The TickerInside ETF dataset uses CC BY 4.0 attribution; Yahoo/yfinance
  price and stock data have separate personal-use/data-licensing limits.
- Different issuer and SEC N-PORT dates can make overlap estimates stale.
- A stock's reported company country is not its revenue distribution,
  investment exposure geography, or currency sensitivity.
- ETF sector/country values are FUND-LEVEL provider allocations; these do
  not automatically provide each constituent's individual industry.
- `--strict-exposure` is a policy for detailed exposure ONLY; separate
  V1–V6 performance and risk workflows are not thereby disabled.
- Per-asset `price_risk_status: not_checked_here` is deliberate in the
  exposure command; price coverage is measured in separate audits.
- Current `asset_support.instrument_kind` for ETFs may describe the
  UNDERLYING HOLDINGS kind (e.g. "stock" for QQQ), not the listing type.
  The portfolio asset is still an ETF; interpret that field carefully in
  downstream UI until its name/meaning is standardized.
- The remaining six parser errors and unverified issuer categories are
  recorded limitations rather than silently treating them as valid equity.

CLOSEOUT AND NEXT VERSION
-------------------------
V7 is complete for the deliberately bounded exposure scope. It provides
usable, source-dated stock/ETF exposure analysis when the free providers
can justify it, labels incomplete coverage, and refuses unsupported
constituents in strict mode. It does not promise full international ETF
holdings, universal quote history, bond analytics or derivatives.

V8 will address fundamental company valuation as a DISTINCT module.
No V7 holdings or company classifications should be interpreted as
forward earnings, fair value, or intrinsic value forecasts.

PURPOSE
-------
V1–V5 established validated data ingestion, currency-aware accounting,
performance analysis, historical backtesting and portfolio-risk modelling.
V6 adds rules for setting and periodically recalculating portfolio target
weights, and compares the consequences of those rules on the SAME eligible
historical periods and assumptions.

IMPLEMENTED STRATEGIES
----------------------
1. input_buy_hold: Invest at user-supplied initial proportions, then allow
   weights to drift.
2. input_rebalanced: Periodically restore the supplied proportions.
3. equal_weight: Assign equal weights across the supplied assets.
4. inverse_vol: Weight holdings inversely to trailing volatility.
5. min_variance: Use a shrinkage-adjusted covariance estimate to solve a
   constrained long-only minimum-variance allocation.
6. momentum_top_k: Rotate equally into the top K assets ranked using a
   specified historical momentum lookback and skip interval.

IMPLEMENTATION AND ASSUMPTIONS
------------------------------
- Supports monthly or quarterly rebalancing as exercised in tests (calendar
  transitions use the first shared close of the new period).
- Allocation signals use only closes THROUGH THE PREVIOUS common date.
- Supports configurable initial capital, fees in basis points, risk-free
  assumption, minimum-history/lookback settings, maximum holding weight,
  benchmark comparisons and a later-period analytical breakdown.
- Uses CAD base currency in the recorded runs, including necessary FX
  conversion; assets share a common valuation-date calendar.
- Uses adjusted-close total-return proxies and fractional fills; not an
  executable, tax-aware, spread-aware, real brokerage trading simulation.
- Assets in the example portfolio were selected retrospectively. Rebalance
  signal timing is controlled, but universe selection can still introduce
  hindsight/survivorship bias.

FINDINGS AND WHAT THEY DO NOT PROVE
-----------------------------------
- Minimum variance had the strongest risk-adjusted results in the reported
  example windows, with lower volatility and shallower drawdowns than the
  other tested strategies.
- Momentum's highest CAGR in the first window came with markedly higher
  volatility, deeper drawdowns and far greater transaction costs; in the
  different monthly/later-window analysis its performance deteriorated.
- These differences illustrate the returns-versus-risk tradeoff. They do
  not establish that a rule will outperform in live investments.
- The simulated strategies use a retrospectively chosen asset universe,
  simplified closes and historical samples with limited market regimes.
- Realism is bounded by missing spreads, slippage, taxes, corporate action
  intricacies, brokerage constraints and potential data-provider issues.
